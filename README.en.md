# Evidence-bound risk fields: a contract-review dataset

[中文](README.md)

This repository releases the de-identified contract-review dataset used in the paper *Evidence-bound risk fields: separating evidence assessment from rule computation in contract review*: the real-material evaluation set, professional training tasks, the synthetic tasks of both experiments and the student-model data. It contains data and annotations only; models, code, model outputs and training logs are not released.

## Real-material evaluation set

A review question contains a set of materials, one review task and several sub-questions; each separately scored sub-question is a scored item. Questions from the same transaction or closely related materials form a source group (field `source_family`). In `gold_keys.jsonl`, the 10 questions with `split` = `development` are development questions (30 items); the 18 `test_reserved` questions are the paper's 17 formal questions (53 items; SOURCE-GROUP-02, 03, 05, 06 and 07) and one separately reported judgment-reading question (`task_type` = `judgment_reading`, 4 items). Source groups G02–G07 in the paper are SOURCE-GROUP-02 to SOURCE-GROUP-07 here.

Read `public_inputs.jsonl` and `gold_keys.jsonl` separately and do not expose reference answers to the model.

## Synthetic tasks

The 8 variants of the Experiment 1 new-expression cases (field `variant`) are `base`, `paraphrase`, `template_only` (promise only), `redacted_original`, `wrong_actor`, `wrong_time`, `irrelevant` and `conflict`. Scene groups 1–32 in Supplementary Table S4 of the paper correspond to SHIFT-000 to SHIFT-031.

Parties, matters and rules in the synthetic scenes are fictional; reference states are generated by program from the stated rule and are not legal judgments (field `label_authority` records the label source of each record).

## Record format

Each non-empty JSONL line is one record; JSON files hold arrays or records grouped into collections. `target`, `states`, gold `bindings` and reference answers are for supervision or scoring and must be kept separate from model inputs at test time. The same facts may appear in several wordings; count independent samples by group fields such as `pair_group` or `family`, and keep the given training, development and test splits.

Record identifiers and group fields keep the identifiers assigned during data construction (prefixes such as `SHIFT-`, `V8-` and `R2-` to `R11-` mark construction batches), so that cross-file references remain valid.

## De-identification

Names, organisations and sources use stable aliases, consistent across contracts, questions and reference answers; direct contact details, addresses, bank accounts, registration numbers, contract identifiers and case numbers have been processed. Dates, amounts, ratios and logical relations needed for the tasks are retained. The de-identified text differs from the original experimental inputs, so training or inference on it should be reported as a new experiment. Retained amounts and dates may still allow linkage to sources; removing direct identifiers is not proof of irreversible anonymisation.

## Not included

Model weights and adapters, feature caches, model outputs, training logs, code, identity mappings, original contract attachments and signed expert originals are not released.
